Dumpster Tips

1

Pick the Right Size

It's better to size up a little than to run out of room mid-project. When in doubt, call us and describe the job — we'll point you to the size that saves you money.

2

Load It Evenly

Distribute heavy items across the bottom and keep the load level. Don't fill above the top edge — an overloaded dumpster can't be safely or legally hauled.

3

Keep the Path Clear

Make sure there's room for the truck to drop off and pick up. Move vehicles and low branches ahead of time so delivery day goes smoothly.

4

Separate the No-Gos

Set aside hazardous materials, tires, and batteries before you start. Knowing what can't go in keeps your pickup on schedule and avoids extra fees.

5

Break Down Bulky Items

Flattening boxes and breaking apart furniture frees up a surprising amount of space, so you get more out of every container.

6

Book Ahead When You Can

Scheduling a day or two early helps us get you the size you want on the date you need. Last-minute jobs are welcome too — just call.
